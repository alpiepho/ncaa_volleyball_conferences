  • Sources: Niche, Massey Ratings, the conference's own standings page, WICHE's WUE savings finder, IPEDS, and each school's own athletics site. Full pull log in data/2026/logs/WCC_RESULTS.md.
  • All 10 WCC members are private universities, so every cost is that school's flat private tuition; no residency or WUE calculation applies.
